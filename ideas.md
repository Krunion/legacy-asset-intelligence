## Selected Approach: Corporate Clarity (Approach 2)

**Design Movement:** Corporate Modernism — inspired by premium consulting firm annual reports and financial intelligence platforms. Clean, authoritative, and data-forward.

**Core Principles:**
1. Data-forward: Charts and metrics are the heroes; prose supports them.
2. Structured hierarchy: Clear typographic scale guides the eye from headline to detail.
3. Controlled color: A restrained palette of deep slate, teal, and warm amber avoids visual noise.
4. Purposeful whitespace: Breathing room signals confidence and professionalism.

**Color Philosophy:**
- Primary: Deep Slate Blue (#1E3A5F) — authority, trust, intelligence
- Accent: Emerald Teal (#0D9488) — growth, recovery, precision
- Highlight: Warm Amber (#F59E0B) — capital, value, urgency
- Background: Off-white (#F8FAFC) — clean, professional
- Text: Charcoal (#1E293B) — readable, serious

**Layout Paradigm:** Asymmetric editorial layout. Left-rail navigation anchors the page; content flows in a two-column grid with full-width data visualization sections. Avoids centered-everything generic layouts.

**Signature Elements:**
1. Horizontal rule dividers with a teal left-border accent on section headers
2. Metric cards with large bold numbers and subtle gradient backgrounds
3. Animated progress bars for market share and financial data

**Interaction Philosophy:** Scroll-triggered section reveals; hover states on chart data points; smooth tab transitions between sections.

**Animation:** Subtle entrance animations (fade + slide up, 200-300ms) for section content. Counter animations for key statistics. Chart animations on first render.

**Typography System:**
- Display: "Playfair Display" (serif) — for hero headlines and section titles
- Body: "Source Sans 3" (sans-serif) — for body text, labels, and data
- Mono: "JetBrains Mono" — for financial figures and data points

**Brand Essence:** Legacy Asset Intelligence — for enterprises that can't afford invisible losses. Authoritative. Precise. Relentless.

**Brand Voice:** Headlines are direct and data-driven. CTAs are action-oriented. No filler. Example: "15–30% of your assets don't exist. We find them." / "Recover capital. Govern with confidence."

**Wordmark & Logo:** A stylized "LAI" monogram with a ghost-like transparency effect on the "A" — representing the invisible asset made visible.

**Signature Brand Color:** Deep Slate Blue (#1E3A5F) — unmistakably LAI's.

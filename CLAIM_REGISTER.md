## Professional Scope Boundaries

LAI does NOT:
- Perform audits or provide assurance/attestation opinions
- Provide financial advice, tax advice, or legal counsel
- Guarantee specific dollar recovery amounts
- Perform property appraisals or valuations
- Act as a CPA firm or licensed appraiser

LAI DOES:
- Provide consulting services for fixed asset record reconciliation
- Help organizations identify discrepancies between records and physical assets
- Develop governance frameworks and accountability programs
- Support organizations in preparing documentation for their auditors
- Provide directional estimates of potential financial exposure

## Update Process

1. Before adding any new factual claim to the website, document it here with source
2. If a claim cannot be sourced to a verifiable publication, use qualifying language ("industry research suggests", "organizations typically find")
3. All financial projections must include disclaimer language
4. Case studies must be labeled as illustrative scenarios unless based on actual engagement results
5. Review this register quarterly for accuracy
